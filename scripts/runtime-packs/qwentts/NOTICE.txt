tools/sigil-tts-server.cpp is Sigil's server program, derived from qwentts.cpp's
tools/tts-server.cpp (MIT): it adds an API key, the loopback-only bind, the extracted voice
latents in the registration answer and an info route. The full license texts are in
THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt.

The models it runs (Qwen3-TTS 12 Hz talkers and the 12 Hz tokenizer, Alibaba / Qwen team,
Apache 2.0, as GGUF from https://huggingface.co/Serveurperso/Qwen3-TTS-GGUF) are separate
downloads.
