# Whisper whole-utterance hallucinations: English (also checked for every other language).
#
# Format (all phrase files):
# - One phrase per line; '#' starts a comment. Matching ignores case, punctuation and spaces, and
#   happens against WHOLE sentences only: an utterance is dropped when every sentence in it matches
#   a phrase. A phrase inside longer real speech ("thanks for watching my back") is never removed.
# - A trailing '*' matches any sentence that starts with the phrase ("subtitles by*").
# - A leading '~' marks a soft phrase: something people really say (streamers do end with "thanks
#   for watching"), so it is dropped only when Whisper itself doubted the audio held speech
#   (no-speech probability >= 0.5; hallucinations on noise score about 0.9, real short words <= 0.47).
# - Hard phrases (no '~') are dropped at any no-speech probability: keep them to credit lines
#   nobody says out loud (subtitle/caption credits, broadcaster tags).
# Curated from the Bag-of-Hallucinations study and field reports; deliberately NOT the raw list,
